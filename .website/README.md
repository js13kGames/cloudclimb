## ☁️ Cloudclimb

Clicky-battler where you climb an endless stack of clouds, battling a Stormling
on every one for loot and Magic Dust.

### How to play

- Tap **Magic** to climb and auto-battle the Stormling on the next cloud.
- Win loot and Magic Dust every fight, win or lose.
- Tap an item to inspect it or spend Magic Dust to upgrade it.
- Compare new drops against your gear before equipping.
- Climb deeper for tougher enemies and better odds at rare, epic, and legendary loot.
